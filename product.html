Popular Monstera Varieties
• Monstera deliciosa: The classic, large-leafed variety with deep splits and holes that can grow quite massive.
• Monstera adansonii: Also called the "Monkey Mask," a smaller vining species with closed holes in the leaves rather than open slits.
• Variegated Cultivars: Highly sought-after collector plants like the Monstera Albo Variegata (white and green splashes) and Thai Constellation (creamy speckles).
